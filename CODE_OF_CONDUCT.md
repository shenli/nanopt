# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

NanoPT contributors commit to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.
Examples of positive behavior include giving actionable feedback, acknowledging uncertainty,
crediting prior work, and focusing criticism on ideas and evidence.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private
information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact process in `SECURITY.md`. Project
maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior threatens the safety or effectiveness of the community. Maintainers
will handle reports as confidentially and impartially as practical.
